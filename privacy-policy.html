What we collect

CineDhol itself does not require accounts and does not store personal information on our servers. The site is static: your browser talks directly to third-party APIs (The Movie Database) to load movie data.

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to personalise ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Learn more in Google's advertising privacy policy.
